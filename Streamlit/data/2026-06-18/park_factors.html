Temperatures will range from high 60s to 90° for Thursday's smaller slate of games, which will feature high wind speeds at most venues. This includes Fenway, Sutter Health Park, and Yankee Stadium - all of which are expecting 15+ mph blowing out to center field. All three stadiums are receptive to wind, and will pair with low air pressure on Thursday to produce an extra boost to fly ball distance.

A 15-mph wind will only blow out slightly in Philadelphia this evening (it's mostly left-to-right), but hitters will still benefit from high 80s which, paired with low air pressure on Thursday, will make the small outfield at Citizens Bank even more vulnerable to homers than usual.

Temperatures are cooler in the Midwest on Thursday as it will be around 70° in both Kansas City and Milwaukee. The double-digit wind blowing out for Guardians / Brewers can be ignored as American Family Field isn't very receptive to wind.

The games in Boston and Atlanta have been flagged for possible delay/PPD with rain expected in the area.

Rates are shrunk toward each batter's 2026+2025 season rates using a Beta-Binomial empirical-Bayes posterior with prior strength k=60 PA: p_post = (α + x) / (α + β + n) where α = season_rate · k, β = (1−season_rate) · k, and (x, n) are observed BvP successes/trials.

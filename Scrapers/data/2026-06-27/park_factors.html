Last Updated: 9:31 AM

Fairly mild weather is expected on Saturday with 60s and 70s expected for most outdoor games. It will be the warmest in St. Louis where the usually pitcher-friendly Busch Stadium will play on the hitter's side of neutral with mid-80s expected throughout.

The most impactful wind on Saturday is in Boston and Cleveland where it will blow in from right at around 10 mph. Home runs will be a bit tougher to come by at these two venues as both are receptive to in-blowing wind. Similar wind is expected in Minnesota this evening, which isn't quite as receptive.

The forecast at Oracle Park is calling for wind blowing out, which it does virtually every game in San Francisco. This can be misleading as the forecast doesn't line up with how the wind behaves on the field, which is swirling and unpredictable. It isn't that the 20+ wind won't be impactful (although the stadium is designed to suppress it), but rather that it's difficult to predict which way it will be blowing inside the park at any given moment. Oracle is a bottom-five park for homers each year despite the "always out" wind forecast.

Today's games in Baltimore and Pittsburgh are flagged for possible delay/PPD due to a chance of rain.

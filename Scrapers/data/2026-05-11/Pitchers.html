Projections

Projections are averages from Ballpark Pal's full game simulations. Rather than predicting any single stat in isolation, the sim plays each MLB game out from the first pitch forward — 3,000 times — and averages the results. Every value shown here (PA, HR, H, K, BB) falls out naturally from that process, grounded in the full context of the game: lineups, matchups, park, and weather.

Grades

Matchup — The letter grade beside each batter shows how favorable the matchup is from the chosen perspective. It's the output of Ballpark Pal's Matchup Model, a machine learning model trained on over a million plate appearances that produces the full probability distribution used by the sims.

Pitch Arsenal — In Pitch Arsenal view, each cell rates how a hitter handles one specific pitch type. Every pitch he's faced is re-scored by our pitch expectation model with park, weather, and luck effects stripped out — isolating true skill vs. that pitch, independent of results or context.

Temperatures will tick up on Thursday with all seven outdoor venues expecting temps in the 70s and higher. The warmest games will be on the East Coast where it will be above 80° in Boston, Atlanta, New York, and Philadelphia.

While warm temps increase fly ball distance, high pressure does the opposite as the denser air makes it more difficult for the ball to travel. Citizens Bank is still rated positive for homers and run scoring this afternoon while Yankee Stadium is closer to neutral (although the short porch in right field is still a prime target for cheap home runs).

Fenway Park is rated the top venue for run scoring on Thursday where it will be in the mid-80s with light wind blowing out. Most of the extra damage in Fenway comes from hits inside the yard, but the weather has it playing on the hitter's side of neutral for homers this afternoon.
